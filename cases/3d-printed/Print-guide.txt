THE CONTRAPTION SHUFFLE
Three-bay card case | v1.0 | M.B. Parks

PRINT TWO PIECES
Import Base.stl and Lid.stl into your slicer as separate parts. Use millimetres
and 100% scale. Print one of each. The base opens upward. The lid's lettered
exterior lies on the bed and its hollow side opens upward. Both files are
already in these orientations.

Suggested starting profile for a 0.4 mm nozzle: 0.20 mm layers, 3 wall loops,
4 top and bottom layers, and 15% infill. PLA or PETG is suitable. Use your
printer's material profile. The two parts can print without supports in the
provided orientations. The shallow elastic-band tracks bridge 6.6 mm. The
recessed title takes the surface finish of your build plate around its letters.
The preview uses two filament colors. Single-color printing works as well.

LOAD THE CASE
With the M.B. PARKS lettering facing you, place one 18-card pile on its long
edge in each bay. From front to back: Materials, Mechanism, Mission. The right
end has a stack of sheets, a gear and a flag in the same order. For the usual
draw, place category backs facing you. To lift out a full pile, grip its exposed
short ends. About 24.7 mm of each pile projects above the open case.

Lower the cap straight onto the shoulder. It is a clearance-fit lid and lifts
off by hand. To secure the case in a bag, stretch a flat elastic band up to
6 mm wide around the top and bottom in either pair of aligned end tracks.
The elastic is optional and supplied separately. The cap has no snap latch.

FIT AND DIMENSIONS (mm)
Finished unsleeved card: 88.9 long x 63.5 high (3.5 x 2.5 in).
Each bay: 90.9 long x 10.8 wide. Designed for an 18-card stack measuring up
to 9.0 thick, leaving 1.8 for handling. Measure your thickest 18-card stack,
especially if the cards are laminated or glued. Closed headroom: 1.8.
Closed case: 99.5 long x 44.2 deep x 70.1 high.
Base: 99.5 x 44.2 x 41.2. Lid: 99.5 x 44.2 x 38.5.
Lid engagement: 9.6. Sliding clearance: 0.30 per side.
Nominal walls: 2.0 at neck and cap, 1.6 for dividers, 2.4 for floor and roof.
Recessed lettering and band tracks locally reduce those thicknesses.
Each STL fits a 100 x 45 mm footprint before any brim or slicer margin.

EDIT THE SIZE
Base.step and Lid.step are separate solid models in their closed assembly
positions. Use them directly in a CAD editor. The STL files are in print
positions, so the lid is flipped relative to the STEP model.

The parametric source is model.py (Python 3 and CadQuery 2.7). Change
PACK_THICKNESS to your measured stack, or change CARD_LENGTH and CARD_HEIGHT
to the outside size of your sleeves. LID_PLAY adjusts printer fit per side.
Run python3 model.py to regenerate both parts. Re-slice the two files together
after changing dimensions. The engraved marks are modeled into the solids.

VERIFICATION
Both exported STLs were checked for closed edges, consistent normals, a
single connected body, positive volume and a flat build orientation. The CAD
solids were checked with three 9.0 mm dummy stacks and along the cap removal
path. Fit has been checked digitally. A physical print has not been tested.
