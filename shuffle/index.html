      The workshop rules
      • The Materials card names your main building material, not your only material.
      • Ordinary glue, tape, string, and fasteners are allowed unless the twist says otherwise.
      • Mechanism-specific extras are listed on the card and in your brief.
      • The mechanism or physical principle must help accomplish the mission.
      • A working small-scale model counts. Creative interpretations are encouraged.
      • Workshop mode applies permissive compatibility rules. Wild mode skips those rules; both respect supply selections.
